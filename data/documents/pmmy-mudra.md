## Who may apply
PMMY serves non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises in manufacturing, trading and services, including allied agriculture activities. It covers new and existing units. Loans come from banks, small finance banks, RRBs, MFIs and NBFCs.

## Benefits
Loans are grouped as Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh), Tarun (above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh, for borrowers who repaid an earlier Tarun loan).

## Documents
Lenders ask for a loan application form, identity and address proof, and business details. Equipment loans need supplier details, and larger expansion loans may need a viability report. Exact requirements differ by lender.

## How to apply
Apply at a participating lender's branch, or online through portals such as Udyamimitra or JanSamarth where available. The lender appraises each proposal and sets its own terms.
