## Who may apply
The applicant should be a resident of Tamil Nadu for not less than 3 years and a first-generation entrepreneur. There is no income ceiling. Assistance is available only for new projects sanctioned under NEEDS. Applicants who have received assistance under UYEGP, TAHDCO or any other central or state scheme are not eligible, and the applicant should not be a defaulter to a commercial bank or TIIC. Partnership concerns may be considered if every partner meets the criteria.

## Benefits
An additional 10% capital subsidy applies for enterprises promoted by SC/ST or differently-abled candidates. Collateral security is decided as per the bank or TIIC guidelines.
