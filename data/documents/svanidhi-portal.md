## Who may apply
The scheme is for street vendors. Vendors are identified by a certificate of vending or ID card, or by a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the urban local body. Vendors in census towns and peri-urban areas need an LoR approved by the Block Development Officer.

## Benefits
Street vendors can get collateral-free working-capital term loans in tranches, with a larger tranche after timely repayment. Tranche amounts have changed since launch, so confirm the current amounts on the portal.

## Documents
A certificate of vending or an LoR issued through the portal is needed for vendors without a vending certificate. Lenders also ask for KYC documents, an application and an undertaking letter.

## How to apply
Apply through the PM SVANidhi portal. Lenders and assisting bodies can help with the application.
