## Who may apply
The page lists any individual above 18 years of age as an eligible beneficiary. Units that have already availed a government subsidy under another central or state scheme are not eligible.

## Benefits
Funding is shared between the beneficiary's own contribution, a margin-money subsidy and a bank term loan for the balance. Subsidy rates depend on the beneficiary category and on whether the unit is in an urban or rural area: general category 15% urban and 25% rural; special categories 25% urban and 35% rural. The beneficiary's own contribution is 10% for the general category.
